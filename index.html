      Step oneChoose a city

      Step twoPlant a seed

      Start a pomodoro. The seed grows for as long as you stay focused. Wander off, and it wilts.

      After each one, write a line. Collect stamps, press your plants in the journal and watch the seasons turn.

      Your garden, journal and stamps are kept in this browser, on this device. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere.
